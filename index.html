About Dezuri

A comprehensive response to evolving construction needs

Dezuri is a construction and services provision company providing leading, sustainable, integrated solutions in planning, engineering, consultancy, project management and real estate development.

Established in 2015 as a limited liability company, Dezuri is focused on providing reliable and cost-effective solutions for individual, not-for-profit and corporate clients. Dezuri endeavours to meet the needs of its customers and satisfy them beyond measure while endlessly pursuing perfection in what it does.

Our multidisciplinary team brings together experience in engineering, general management and project management, with an understanding of how design, resource management, timely construction and cost must work together.

Residential construction

Homes built with durability, quality and peace of mind

A house is often the most important asset a client will ever have. Dezuri works to ensure it is properly built, structurally sound and handed down in good condition to future generations.

Building a house can at times be a source of stress. Dezuri is here to help clients better use their time while building an important long-term asset. House renovation projects also breathe new life into tired homes, creating better spaces, structural improvements and modernised finishes.

  • Experienced and honest professionals
  • Attention to the seen and unseen details
  • Good-quality materials from reputable suppliers
  • Customer communication and involvement throughout execution
  • External review and monitoring by an architect and structural engineer
  • Construction methods that keep maintenance requirements low
